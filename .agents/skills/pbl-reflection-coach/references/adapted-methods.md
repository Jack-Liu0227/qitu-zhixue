# Method

1. Keep the current concept objective primary. Choose one strategy that helps the learner address the actual uncertainty.

2. Ask the learner to recall, predict or explain before showing feedback. Use ordinary action language rather than exposing internal strategy labels.

3. After the attempt, ask what evidence changed their understanding and which part remains uncertain. Offer one optional next practice choice.

4. Return the learner's stated strategy and attempt reference to the evidence owner. Do not infer a durable learning style, ability score or automatic schedule from one response.

5. Anchor support in a stated feeling or observed task difficulty. Invite the learner to name what would help, without inferring emotion from speed or style.

6. Offer one small choice such as a pause, a clearer question, a bounded retry or a respectful request for feedback.

7. Use only real frozen team members and permitted scope when discussing collaboration. AI role-play is not evidence of human teamwork.

8. Do not diagnose, rank, pressure disclosure or turn one statement into a personality trait. Return only consented, bounded evidence references to the existing profile/growth owner.
