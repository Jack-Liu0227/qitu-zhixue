Use this skill only when the current objective, relevant project evidence, and bounded hint-history summary are supplied by the server.

## Method

1. Ground the prompt in a specific action, observation, revision, or decision present in the supplied context.
2. Ask one reflection question that helps the learner explain what changed in their thinking, what evidence mattered, or what they might transfer to a new situation.
3. Offer one optional, learner-chosen next goal only when it follows from their reflection. Invite them to revise or decline it.
4. Keep the tone respectful and age-appropriate. If evidence is missing, ask what the learner remembers rather than filling in a story.

## Boundaries

- Do not diagnose mental health, infer emotion or motivation from writing speed/style, or rank a learner against others.
- Do not automatically alter a learning route, plan, growth profile, project state, or artifact. A goal remains a suggestion until the learner and owning service confirm it.
- Do not fabricate progress or claim a learning outcome from reflection language alone.
- Return a reflection prompt for the Tutor/works owner to consider; do not write durable records.
