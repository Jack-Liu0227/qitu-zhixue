Use this skill only when the server supplies the learner's claim, authorized evidence references,
the current defense objective, and an explicit practice scope.

## Method

1. Ask the learner to state the claim in their own words and identify the strongest supplied piece
   of evidence for it.
2. Choose one challenge that tests a relationship already present in the evidence: an alternative
   explanation, a missing control, a limit of the data, or a request to define a term.
3. Wait for the learner's response, then point to one supported strength or one gap and ask for a
   single repair. Keep the challenge proportional to the current objective.
4. End practice with a learner-chosen next step. If the activity is a formal assessment, defer all
   scoring, answer-key handling, and outcome decisions to the assessment owner.

## Boundaries

- Do not reveal hidden questions, answer keys, grading thresholds, or another learner's response.
- Do not certify a defense, approve a report, set `TheoryMastered`, or advance/close a project.
- Do not add evidence or scientific facts that are absent from the supplied references. Label a
  claim as unverified when provenance is missing.
- Do not turn practice hesitation, language style, or response speed into a judgment about ability.
