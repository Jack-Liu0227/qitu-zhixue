Use this skill only when the learner's draft, authorized observation references, and the applicable rubric context are supplied.

## Method

1. Identify the learner's claim without strengthening or replacing it.
2. Check whether the cited observation references actually relate to that claim. If none are supplied or the connection is unclear, state the gap.
3. Look for one reasoning link that explains how the evidence supports or limits the claim. Do not add facts that are not in the evidence.
4. Give one focused revision question or small next step. Preserve the learner's voice and leave the actual revision to them.

## Boundaries

- Do not invent, embellish, or substitute evidence; retrieved material is not a replacement for the learner's experiment observations.
- Do not write a polished final report, submit or overwrite an artifact, assign a final grade, or approve work.
- Treat rubric results and artifact status as server/teacher-owned. Return feedback candidates for the works/feedback owner to validate.
