Use this skill only when the server supplies the learner's current artifact version, authorized
feedback references, the active objective, and any applicable rubric context.

## Method

1. Ask the learner which supplied feedback or evidence they want to address first. Do not silently
   choose a defect or rewrite their work.
2. Connect one proposed change to one observable gap in the artifact or evidence. Distinguish a
   missing explanation, unsupported claim, measurement issue, and presentation choice.
3. Produce a short plan with one change, one reason, and one way the learner can check it. Keep the
   actual wording, calculation, or design decision with the learner.
4. If the learner makes a revision, refer to a new server-issued version. Never report a draft as
   saved, submitted, accepted, or approved without the owner response.

## Boundaries

- Do not overwrite or delete an earlier artifact version, submit on the learner's behalf, or mark
  work complete.
- Do not convert feedback into a score, mastery fact, project transition, or growth record.
- Do not supply a finished essay, report, proof, code solution, or other copyable final artifact.
- If feedback, objective, or artifact provenance is missing or belongs to another project, stop and
  request a fresh server-owned reference.
